MediCare+ 
An advanced, real-time medication tracker and AI adherence system designed to empower patients, streamline prescription management, 
             and keep family caregivers connected through intelligent alerts and safety nets.
About the Project
   MediCare+ is a comprehensive health-tech platform built to tackle medication non-adherence. 
    By combining cutting-edge AI assistance, OCR prescription scanning, real-time database synchronization,
     and emergency safety features, MediCare+ ensures that complex medication schedules are managed safely and effortlessly.
The Problem & Solution
The Problem: Medication non-adherence among elderly and chronically ill patients leads to severe health complications, missed doses, and delayed emergency responses. 
             Family caregivers also lack real-time visibility into patient medication compliance.

The Solution: MediCare+ bridges this gap by digitizing prescriptions via OCR, offering an interactive AI health assistant (Dr. Alex),
              providing automated caregiver alerts for missed doses, and incorporating an instant Emergency SOS safety net.
Key Features
📷 OCR Prescription Scanner: Instantly scan physical prescriptions to digitize medication schedules and avoid manual entry errors.

🤖 Dr. Alex AI Health Assistant: An interactive virtual health assistant ready to answer medication-related questions and guide users through their routines.

⚠️ Automated Caregiver Alerts: Instant notification system that triggers alerts for missed doses, keeping family caregivers informed and proactive.

🚨 Emergency SOS: Quick-access emergency safety trigger built directly into the dashboard for urgent situations.

👁️ Pill Visual Guides: Clear visual references to help users identify the correct pills and dosage forms.

🗣️ Voice-Assisted Reminders: Audio-guided alerts to ensure patients never miss a scheduled medication time.
